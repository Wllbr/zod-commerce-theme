# ZOD Product Page v1.6.1 — Salla-Native Trust & Reviews

This release keeps product, review, rating, installment and payment data owned by Salla while using ZOD styling.

## Review section
- `store.settings.rating.show_on_product` controls whether reviews are shown.
- `salla-reviews-summary` renders the product rating summary from Salla.
- `salla-comments` renders the real product review/question form and comments.
- The theme only controls the surrounding responsive layout.

## Trusted payment
- `salla-payments` renders the payment methods currently enabled for the store.
- The theme does not manually list Visa, Mada, Apple Pay, COD, or other methods.

## Three confidence points
The product purchase area shows three ZOD cards when `show_product_trust` is enabled:
1. Original products
2. Trusted payment
3. Fast delivery

The wording is bilingual. Product/payment functionality remains Salla-native.

## Theme editor controls
- Show product trust features
- Show active payment methods on product page
- Show reviews summary
- Salla dashboard rating setting still controls the overall review section
