# ZOD Commerce v1.6.37

This release uses the approved Orkida-inspired storefront density as the main product-card reference while preserving ZOD branding.

## Product cards
- Six visible cards on wide desktop.
- Eye on the left and Heart on the right.
- Desktop actions appear on hover; mobile actions are always visible.
- Wishlist active state is always ZOD red.
- Brand logo rows are removed from product cards.
- VAT uses Salla's native `pages.products.tax_included` translation when the product is taxable.

## Quick View
The Eye action opens a lightweight overlay with product media, category, price, stock, short copy, quantity, Add to Cart, and a full product-page link.

## Product page
- Smaller desktop title and gallery.
- Slimmer header.
- Persistent purchase dock retained.
- Mobile dock is a compact two-row purchase UI: quantity above, purchase actions below.
