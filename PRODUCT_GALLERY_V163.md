# ZOD Product Gallery v1.6.3

- Discount display now requires a real sale price greater than zero.
- Product images remain sourced from `product.images`.
- Thumbnails are a floating horizontal rail inside the gallery, centered near the bottom.
- The thumbnail rail can be swiped/scrolled and clicking a thumbnail calls Salla Slider `slideTo()`.
- Main image swiping stays native through `<salla-slider>`.
- Previous/next controls are custom transparent arrow-only buttons using Salla Slider `slidePrev()` / `slideNext()`.
- Mobile shows compact Share and Wishlist controls below the image area.
