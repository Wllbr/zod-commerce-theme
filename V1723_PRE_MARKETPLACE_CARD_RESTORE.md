# v1.7.23 — Pre-marketplace card restore

The prior rollback used v1.7.13, but the changelog shows the marketplace card rebuild actually landed in v1.7.12. Therefore v1.7.13 was not a valid pre-redesign baseline.

This release restores the documented pre-v1.7.12 card behavior while preserving newer product-page compatibility fixes.

Expected visual markers:
- Eye + Heart action cluster on the product image (Eye left, Heart right)
- full-width pill purchase action under the card content
- category pill
- no image-corner plus button
- no gallery dots
- no brand-name row from v1.7.13
- discount/promotion badges in the image corners
- out-of-stock stamp retained

Laser World is intentionally unchanged.
